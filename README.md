# Lift Log

A personal workout tracker that runs on your iPhone like a normal app and works fully offline. It plans your lifts, tells you what weight to use, and logs every set.

App link: https://zachhowks-bit.github.io/lift-log/

## Put it on your iPhone

1. Open the link above in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. From now on, open Lift Log from its icon on your Home Screen.

## Back up your data every week

Your workouts are stored **only on your phone**. If you delete the icon, the data can be deleted too.

- **Back up:** open Lift Log, go to **Settings → Export backup**, then save the file to **Files / iCloud Drive**.
- **Restore:** **Settings → Import backup**, then pick the saved file.
